02User roles and permission matrix

Principals

Three roles are required by the brief. Two more are recommended because they cost almost nothing to define now and are expensive to retrofit: a client-side administrator (so we are not the ones adding and removing a client's own staff), and a non-human service principal (so the calculation engine's actions are attributable and never masquerade as a person).

RoleTypeScopeExists in MVP-1Purpose
CLIENT_USERExternalOne organization; only projects assigned to that organizationYesConfigure options, compare, submit for quote, view preliminary output and status.
CLIENT_ADMINExternalOne organizationYesEverything a client user can do, plus invite and deactivate their own colleagues within the same organization.
INTERNAL_SALESInternal staffAll organizationsYesSales / designer / estimator. Create client orgs and projects, send invitations, review submissions, see internal BOM and catalog detail, create derived revisions, manage quote status.
INTERNAL_ADMINInternal staffAll organizations + platformYesEverything above, plus user and role administration, catalog and rule-pack release, retention and audit export, and the destructive operations.
SERVICE_ENGINENon-humanSystemYesThe derivation engine. Writes derived outputs and audit events; can never authorise, approve, waive or release.
Why the actor type is a separate axis from the role

Organization isolation stops Client A seeing Client B. It does nothing to stop Client A seeing our margin on Client A's own project — those rows legitimately belong to Client A's organization. Two orthogonal dimensions are therefore required: organization_id (which rows) and actor_type ∈ {client, staff, service} (which tables and which fields). Conflating them is the single most common way a product like this leaks. See §14.

Non-negotiable invariants

I-1. No response to a client principal may contain a cost, price, margin, discount, manufacturer part number, catalog page reference, supplier, BOM line, or internal note — at any nesting depth. Asserted by an automated contract test over every route (§16).
I-2. No principal, of any role, may mutate a submitted revision.
I-3. No principal may delete an audit event.
I-4. SERVICE_ENGINE may never approve, waive, release, or grant.
